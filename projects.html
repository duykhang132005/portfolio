Project Highlights

Custom Electrical Guitar and Drum Set for Guitar Hero

  • Engineered a custom hardware gel-key instrument set for Guitar Hero, run through an emulator and built with 3D printing, soldering, and SolidWorks. Showcased at the 2025 Harvard Engineering Fair. You can read more about it here.
  • Redesigned the material composition and weight distribution to prioritize microcontroller-based input functionality over the original marketing design, leading to a 35% latency drop from 36ms to 24ms.
  • Endorsed by the Co-Founder of Guitar Hero.

3D Webpage for Incoming Students

  • Developed a comprehensive digital resource for incoming Harvard undergraduates, featuring links to all relevant First-Year resources and custom LiDAR-scanned 3D models and reconstructed floor plans of rooms (Tinkercad). You can watch the featured project demo.
  • Presented at the CS50 Fair as a featured project before industry representatives (Meta, HRT, Jane Street), inspiring future institutional acceptance page formats.
  • Endorsed by Prof. David Malan.
