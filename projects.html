Guitar Hero: Emulating Electrical Hardware

A custom hardware gel-key instrument set for Guitar Hero, run through an emulator and built with 3D printing, soldering, and SolidWorks. Redesigning the material composition and weight distribution to prioritize microcontroller-based input over the original marketing design cut latency by 35% (36ms to 24ms). Showcased at the 2025 Harvard Engineering Fair and endorsed by the Co-Founder of Guitar Hero.

3D Webpage for Incoming Students

A comprehensive guide for incoming Harvard College students, with links to all relevant First-Year resources and custom LiDAR-scanned 3D models and reconstructed floor plans of rooms (Tinkercad). Presented at the CS50 Fair as a featured project before industry representatives (Meta, HRT, Jane Street) and endorsed by Prof. David Malan, inspiring the format of acceptance pages for future students.
