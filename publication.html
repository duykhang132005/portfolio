Rayleigh Wave Phase Velocities in the Atlantic Upper Mantle

AGU Journals

  • Produced updated Rayleigh wave phase velocity maps and shear velocity cross-sections for Atlantic upper mantle structure.
  • Engineered distributed geospatial and AI-assisted data pipelines to reconstruct wave propagation models using seismic datasets, reproducing and validating prior Atlantic seafloor mapping research, and validated the new diagrams against Colli et al. (2014).
  • Modernized legacy diagram-generation workflows into containerized, reproducible compute modules, integrating physics-informed ML components to accelerate matrix-processing throughput and automate anomaly detection across large seismic datasets.
  • Rebuilt GMT/Python/MATLAB pipelines to modern standards, improving reproducibility of phase velocity diagrams, sensitivity kernels, and model residual maps.
  • Enabled scalable ingestion of newly acquired data through modular pipeline design.
  • Revision code developed under the supervision of Professor Esther James.
