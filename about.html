About Me

I'm a senior student concentrating in Computer Science and Earth & Planetary Sciences at Harvard. My work blends engineering with environmental science, particularly applications to data-driven research on atmospheric and ocean systems.

Outside of work, I sing, play and arrange music of all kinds. You can find me singing choral music online with the Harvard Glee Club across Europe and the United States, or check us out at Sanders Theatre at Harvard for our semesterly performances. Aside from choir, I also sing a cappella with the Harvard Din & Tonics on their international and domestic tours. You can find more info on the Din & Tonics website.
